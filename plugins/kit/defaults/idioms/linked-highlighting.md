## Helps
A selection of nearby items in one view shows where those items fall in the others (Interact p. 34). Munzner calls it a powerful and pervasive interaction idiom, also known as brushing and linking (Interact p. 34). Our reading: it is often the first interaction worth adding to two static views shown side by side.

## Hides
Each juxtaposed view gets a share of the display; two views get half each (Interact p. 42). Our reading: in a dense scatterplot, highlighted marks can be hidden under the others unless they are drawn on top. Our reading: each added view is one more for the reader to keep track of, and the links between views take time to learn.

## Views
Two or more juxtaposed views; the EDV figure has five (Interact p. 34). Linking can run in one direction or both, and both is almost always better (Interact p. 35). Small multiples can be linked the same way, by the analogous item or attribute across views (Interact p. 40).
