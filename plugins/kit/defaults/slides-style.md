# Slide house conventions

The kit's default conventions for slides, on top of `style.md`. To change them, run `kit customize slides-style` and edit your copy; your copy then replaces this file.

1. Slide text follows the word rules of the base voice: no intensifiers, journalistic words, LLM tics or names from the code.
2. Colour is never the only carrier of meaning: anything a colour distinguishes is also labelled or marked.
3. No text smaller than `\small` on content slides. If it doesn't fit, cut or split the slide.
4. What the speaker says goes in `script.md`, not on the slide or in `\note{}`.
